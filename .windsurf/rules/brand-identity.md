# Movely — Brand Identity & Business Model

## Brand name
- **Movely** (previously "Velocity" — now fully replaced)
- Domain: `www.movelygo.com`
- Service: Driver-customer connection platform

> Note: This rule reflects the current brand identity as **Movely**. If project documentation still references older names (e.g. TaxiLink or Velocity), update those documents to use Movely unless the user says otherwise.

## What Movely IS
- Search platform connecting customers with independent drivers
- Provides visibility and discovery tools for drivers
- Direct connection service (no middleman in transactions)
- No booking fees or commissions
- Platform for direct communication between customers and drivers

## What Movely IS NOT
- NOT a verification service (at least initially)
- NOT confirming driver identities or backgrounds
- NOT handling payments or bookings
- NOT a ride-sharing or taxi company

## Customer value proposition
- Search and compare independent drivers in their area
- View detailed profiles: photos, vehicle info, FAQs, reviews
- Contact drivers directly (phone/WhatsApp)
- Customers are responsible for verifying driver credentials
- No platform fees — negotiate directly with drivers

## Driver value proposition
- Professional profile with vehicle image gallery
- SEO optimization for local discovery
- Analytics and profile view tracking
- Availability management tools
- Reach customers actively seeking transportation
- No commissions or fees — keep 100% of earnings
- Full control over pricing and bookings

## Messaging guidelines
- **Transparent**: Be clear that customers verify drivers themselves
- **Empowering**: Focus on direct connection and control
- **Trustworthy**: Professional, reliable platform for discovery
- **Simple**: Easy search, compare, contact workflow
- **No false promises**: Don't claim verification we don't provide

## Key differentiators
1. Direct contact (no middleman)
2. No platform fees
3. Full transparency about what we do/don't provide
4. Tools for driver business growth (SEO, analytics, galleries)
5. Customer-driven verification process

## Copy and UI guidance
- Avoid language that implies Movely verifies, guarantees, or brokers rides/payments.
- Use terms like "discover", "compare", "connect with", and "contact directly".
- Future revenue idea: local business ads near customer locations (do not build unless explicitly requested).
